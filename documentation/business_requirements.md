# Nexora Technologies — Business Requirements

## Business Overview

Nexora Technologies is a fictional B2B SaaS company providing AI-powered business automation solutions to medium-sized and enterprise businesses.

## Business Problem

The sales department manually handles incoming leads, company research, lead qualification, scoring, salesperson assignment, personalized communication and CRM updates.

This creates operational delays, inconsistent qualification and missed sales opportunities.

## Project Objective

Develop a multi-agent AI-powered sales operations system that automates the lead-to-revenue workflow while maintaining human approval controls.

## Expected Outcomes

1. Automated lead intake and information extraction.
2. Company research and enrichment.
3. Intelligent lead qualification.
4. Rule-based lead scoring.
5. Automated salesperson assignment.
6. Personalized email generation.
7. CRM record creation and updating.
8. Follow-up task generation.
9. Quality assurance and exception handling.
10. Complete execution logs and audit trails.

## System Requirements

- Multiple specialized AI agents.
- Centralized orchestration.
- Structured agent communication.
- Persistent workflow state.
- Duplicate detection.
- Retry and error handling.
- Human approval for external actions.
- Monitoring and execution history.

## Success Criteria

The system should process valid test leads from intake to a complete proposed sales action, identify invalid or incomplete records, prevent duplicate CRM entries and maintain traceable execution records.
